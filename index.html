About

Mental disorders are usually treated by pharmacotherapy or psychotherapy. However, there is a global shortage of human resources for delivering such mental health services. In developed countries there are nine psychiatrists per 100,000 people available, while in developing countries there is one psychiatrist per ten million people.

To address this problem, conversational agents have arisen interest in recent years, particularly in psychoeducation, behaviour change and self-help. Healo is a rasa based conversational chatbot developed with help of cognitive behavioural therapy with a view of helping people tackle their daily mental issues.

Healo helps user to deal with issues such as Anxiety, Depression, getting rid of sad thoughts, etc. Healo uses natural language understanding to converse with user. It also maintain a journal to track the progress of users condition. Healo also provides the user with comfort and suggestion for dealing with the issues they are facing.
